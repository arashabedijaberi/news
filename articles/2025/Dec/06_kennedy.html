You can’t inherit character

Published: Dec 6, 20205

[CNN]: The newly remade CDC vaccine committee—stacked with members appointed by HHS Secretary Robert F. Kennedy Jr., an anti-vaccine activist with no medical or scientific qualifications—voted to end the long-standing recommendation for universal hepatitis B vaccination at birth, replacing it with optional “shared decision-making” for infants born to virus-negative mothers. Public health experts warn this shift will confuse parents and doctors, undermine confidence in a vaccine proven safe for decades, and almost certainly increase preventable hepatitis B infections, which are especially dangerous and often lifelong when acquired in infancy. Major medical organizations, former CDC officials, and even physicians who previously supported Kennedy’s appointment condemned the decision as scientifically unfounded, irresponsible, and harmful, noting it reverses decades of progress that cut childhood hepatitis B cases from tens of thousands to only a few dozen per year.
