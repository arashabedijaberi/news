What goes around comes around.

Published: Dec 5, 20205

[ALJAZEERA]: Russian President Vladimir Putin visited India for the first time since the 2022 Ukraine invasion, meeting Prime Minister Narendra Modi to strengthen trade and defense ties. India has continued purchasing Russian oil despite U.S. sanctions, resisting pressure to condemn Russia, and framing its stance as a call for peace through dialogue. The summit also included discussions on additional Russian arms sales, underscoring India’s ongoing economic and strategic support for Moscow amid the conflict.
