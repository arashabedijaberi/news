[FOX NEWS]: A Stanford-led study suggests the shingles vaccine may reduce the risk of dementia and slow its progression. Using a natural birthdate-based cutoff in Wales, researchers found vaccinated older adults were less likely to develop mild cognitive impairment, less likely to die from dementia, and showed benefits even after diagnosis — though lifestyle differences between vaccinated and unvaccinated groups remain a limitation.
