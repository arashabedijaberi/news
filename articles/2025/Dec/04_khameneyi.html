Where there's smoke, there's fire

Published: Dec 4, 20205

[IRNA]: In a speech to thousands of women and girls, Iran’s Supreme Leader emphasized that women are not merely household workers but managers, with equal rights to men in social, political, economic, and spiritual spheres. He highlighted Islam’s recognition of women’s dignity, freedom, and ability to pursue personal and societal development while maintaining family responsibilities and moral values. Using Fatimah al-Zahra as the ultimate model, he illustrated that women can excel in education, governance, professional life, and public service. He contrasted this with Western capitalist culture, which often reduces women to objects or subordinate roles, and stressed that true Islamic principles empower women to lead, innovate, and participate fully in society while safeguarding their honor, rights, and family.
