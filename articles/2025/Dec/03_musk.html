Even a Bare Roof Won’t Get You Home

Published: Dec 3, 2025

President Trump said he still likes Elon Musk, despite their relationship deteriorating over policy disputes. Musk, once a close ally in the Trump administration helping lead the Department of Government Efficiency (DOGE) and actively campaigning for Trump in 2024, fell out with the former president in June after publicly criticizing the "One Big Beautiful Bill Act" and Trump’s electric-vehicle policies, which impacted Tesla. Since then, their interactions have been sporadic, though Musk has occasionally shown support for Trump’s initiatives and attended events with him. The friendship, once cozy and high-profile, remains noticeably strained [FOX NEWS].
